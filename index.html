What this lab does

Test, verify, produce.

01

Test

Single dependencies — fonts, auth, analytics, media, payments — each loaded the way a real product loads it, plus whole platforms like Firebase, Netlify, and Vercel.

02

Verify

Every verdict comes from an Alibaba Cloud Beijing node: curl + dig for reachability, Playwright for what a real browser shows. Not a VPN. Not a guess.

03

Produce

Weekly machine-readable evidence — verdicts, latency, screenshots, downloadable run reports — accumulated as an open data asset since June 2026.
